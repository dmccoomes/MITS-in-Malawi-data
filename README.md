# MITS-in-Malawi-data
Data from manuscript: Intestinal immunohistochemistry and histology relationships with enteric pathogens in a pediatric postmortem study

This repository hosts data used in the above listed manuscript. 

  1. MiM study enrolled children aged 1 week to 4 years who died during inpatient admission to Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital (Blantyre) for acute illness and/or undernutrition
  2. BEECH comprised children <2 years of age from peri-urban Lusaka, Zambia with poor growth refractory to nutritional intervention and without a medically identifiable cause of growth faltering.
  3. NPA included children <18 years of age from Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center and the University of Virginia Medical Center who underwent endoscopic biopsy for clinical pursposes that did not have an esophagogastroduodenal diagnosis, and whose clinical histology report included no pathologic abnormality.
